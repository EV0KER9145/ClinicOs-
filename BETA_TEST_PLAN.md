# ClinicOS — Beta Test Plan & Controlled Clinic Onboarding Strategy

## 1. Beta Program Objective
Validate ClinicOS in **3–5 real independent clinics** across India to measure operational time savings, callback completion rates, lead conversion rates, and receptionist workflow satisfaction.

---

## 2. Beta Timeline & Milestones

### Phase 1: Synthetic Regression & Internal Pilot (Week 1)
- Onboard test clinic accounts with synthetic doctors, patients, leads, and appointments.
- Verify end-to-end workflows: Registration -> Clinic Setup -> Patient Registration -> Enquiry Capture -> Appointment Booking -> No-Show Recovery Automation -> AI Message Drafting -> Follow-up Completion.

### Phase 2: Pilot Clinic Onboarding (Week 2)
- Onboard 3 pilot clinics (e.g. Dental Clinic, Dermatology Practice, General Clinic).
- Configure clinic timezone (`Asia/Kolkata`), doctors list, and staff team accounts.
- Receptionists begin daily operational usage for walk-in patient registration and phone/WhatsApp lead capture.

### Phase 3: Workflow Observation & Feedback (Week 3)
- Collect quantitative feedback on receptionist task completion time.
- Measure no-show recovery follow-up actions and overdue task reduction.

### Phase 4: Production Hardening & Transition (Week 4)
- Address feedback, refine UX micro-interactions, and finalize beta performance metrics.

---

## 3. Success Metrics & KPIs
1. **Receptionist Speed**: New patient registration created in **< 30 seconds**.
2. **Follow-up Completion Rate**: **> 85%** of created follow-up tasks completed or rescheduled before becoming overdue.
3. **No-Show Recovery Rate**: **> 30%** of missed appointment no-shows converted into rescheduled appointments.
4. **App Stability**: Zero native app crashes (**100% crash-free sessions**).
