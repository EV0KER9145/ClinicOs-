# ClinicOS — Beta Tester Guide for Clinic Staff

Welcome to the **ClinicOS Beta Program**! Thank you for partnering with us to test ClinicOS in your practice.

---

## 1. What is ClinicOS?
ClinicOS is an Android-first clinic management and patient retention platform built to help small and medium-sized clinics organize patient records, track enquiry leads, manage doctor appointment schedules, and keep up with patient follow-ups.

**Core Promise**: *"Never lose a patient because you forgot to follow up."*

> **IMPORTANT BETA DISCLAIMER**:
> ClinicOS is an administrative practice-management tool. It is **NOT an Electronic Medical Record (EMR)** system and does **NOT** provide medical diagnoses, treatment recommendations, or clinical decision-making. Please do not record clinical diagnosis data in ClinicOS.

---

## 2. Getting Started & Onboarding

### Step 1: Install the ClinicOS App
1. Download and install the `ClinicOS-beta.apk` on your Android phone or tablet (Android 8.0 or higher).
2. Open ClinicOS and tap **Register Clinic**.

### Step 2: Register Your Clinic & Owner Account
1. Enter your **Clinic Name** (e.g., *Apex Dental Care*), **Clinic Type** (e.g., *Dental*), **Phone Number**, and **Email**.
2. Create your **Owner Account** details (Full Name, Email, Password).
3. Tap **Create Clinic Account**.

### Step 3: Complete Clinic Setup
1. In the initial onboarding screen, confirm your **Timezone** (e.g., `Asia/Kolkata`) and clinic phone.
2. Tap **Complete Setup**. You will be taken directly to your **Action Dashboard**.

## 3. Core Operational Workflows to Test

### A. Add Doctors & Staff Members
- Tap **More** -> **Doctors** -> tap **+ Add Doctor**. Enter doctor name and specialty.
- Tap **Staff & Receptionists** -> tap **+ Add Staff** to invite receptionists.

### B. Register Patients
- Tap **Patients** in the bottom navigation.
- Tap **+ Add Patient**. Enter **Full Name** (required) and **Phone Number**.
- Add tags (e.g. `VIP`, `Referral`) and administrative notes.

### C. Capture Enquiry Leads
- Tap **Leads** in the bottom navigation -> tap **+ Capture Enquiry**.
- Record new enquiries from phone calls, WhatsApp, Instagram, or walk-ins.
- Set lead stage (`NEW`, `CONTACTED`, `INTERESTED`).
- When a lead decides to visit, tap **Convert Lead to Patient**.

### D. Schedule & Manage Appointments
- Tap **Appts** in the bottom navigation -> tap **+ Book Appointment**.
- Select a patient, doctor, appointment date, and time.
- ClinicOS automatically checks doctor availability and prevents double booking (**Doctor Conflict Warning**).
- After consultation, tap the appointment to update status (`Confirmed`, `Completed`, or `No-Show`).

### E. Follow-ups Task Queue
- Tap **More** -> **Follow-ups Queue** or tap **+ Task** on the Dashboard.
- Schedule callbacks for patient reviews or missed appointment recovery.
- View **Overdue Tasks ⚠** and tap **Mark Completed** when finished.

### F. AI Assistant & Prepared Draft Messages
- Tap **More** -> **AI Assistant Workspace**.
- View **Action Suggestions** for overdue tasks or no-show recoveries.
- Tap **Draft Message** to generate a friendly outreach message. Review, edit, and tap **Copy Message** to paste into WhatsApp or SMS.
- Switch to **Ask ClinicOS** to ask operational questions like *"How many follow-ups are overdue?"*

## 4. How to Report Bugs & Feedback
If you encounter an error, blank screen, or confusing interaction:
1. Note what action you were performing.
2. Go to **More** -> tap **Send Feedback** or contact our beta support team.
3. Include your clinic name, phone model, and a brief description of the issue.
